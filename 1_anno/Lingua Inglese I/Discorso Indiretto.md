# Discorso Indiretto (Reported Speech)

---

## Cos'è il discorso indiretto?

È il modo di **riportare** ciò che qualcuno ha detto, con altre parole e senza le virgolette.

| Diretto | Indiretto |
|---|---|
| She said, "**I am tired**." | She said that **she was tired**. |
| He said, "**I will call you**." | He said that **he would call** me. |

---

## Regola generale: il *backshift*

Quando riportiamo, **spostiamo i tempi verbali di un passo indietro**.

| Tempo nel discorso diretto | Diventa | Nome |
|---|---|---|
| Present Simple | **Past Simple** | backshift |
| Present Continuous | **Past Continuous** | backshift |
| Past Simple | **Past Perfect** | backshift |
| Present Perfect | **Past Perfect** | backshift |
| **will** | **would** | backshift |
| **can** | **could** | backshift |
| **may** | **might** | backshift |
| **must** | **had to** | backshift |
| should | (nessun cambiamento) | invariato |
| would | (nessun cambiamento) | invariato |
| Past Continuous | Past Perfect Continuous | backshift |

---

## Cambio delle formule

| Diretto | Indiretto |
|---|---|
| **now** | **then** / at that moment |
| **today** | **that day** |
| **tomorrow** | **the next day** |
| **yesterday** | **the day before** |
| **here** | **there** |
| **this** | **that** |
| **these** | **those** |
| **last week** | **the week before** |
| **next year** | **the following year** |
| **ago** | **before** |

---

## Il "that" può essere omesso

Nelle frasi brevi il *that* si omette:

> She said (that) **she was** tired.
> He told me (that) **he had** finished.

---

## *Say* e *tell*

| Verbo | Uso | Esempio |
|---|---|---|
| **say** | con o senza destinatario | She **said** (that) she was busy. |
| **tell** | SEMPRE con destinatario | She **told me** (that) she was busy. |

> ⚠️ ~~He said me~~ ❌ → ✅ **He told me** / **He said to me**

---

## Domande

In discorso indiretto **non si invertono** i verbi: non c'è né punto d'interrogativo né *do/does/did*.

| Diretto | Indiretro |
|---|---|
| "**Where** is the file?" | He asked **where the file was**. |
| "**Do** you know C++?" | She asked **if I knew C++**. |
| "**Have** you finished?" | He asked **whether I had finished**. |

### Le congiunzioni interrogative

| Domanda | Indiretto |
|---|---|
| Yes/No | **if** / **whether** |
| What | **what** |
| Where | **where** |
| When | **when** |
| Why | **why** |
| How | **how** |
| Who | **who** |

---

## Ordinali, espressioni e punti cardinali

| Diretto | Indiretro |
|---|---|
| the first of June | **the first of June** (invariato) |
| yesterday | **the day before** |
| next week | **the following week** |
| two days ago | **two days before** |
| a lot of | **a lot of** (invariato) |
| £20 | **twenty pounds** |

---

## Imperativi e frasi con *let's*

| Diretto | Indiretro |
|---|---|
| "**Close** the door." | He told me **to close** the door. |
| "**Let's** go." | He suggested **going**. |
| "**Don't** touch it." | He told me **not to touch** it. |
| "**You should** rest." | He advised me **to rest**. |

---

## Esercizi completi

| Diretto | Indiretto |
|---|---|
| "I am working on a project." | He said he **was working** on a project. |
| "I will send the file tomorrow." | She said she **would send** the file **the next day**. |
| "The server crashed last night." | He said the server **had crashed the night before**. |
| "I have finished the assignment." | She said she **had finished** the assignment. |
| "Can you help me?" | He asked **if I could help** him. |
| "Where do you live?" | She asked **where I lived**. |
| "Please close the window." | He told me **to close** the window. |
| "The exam was difficult." | She said the exam **had been** difficult. |
