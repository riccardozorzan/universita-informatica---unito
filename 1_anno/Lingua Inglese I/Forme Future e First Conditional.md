# Forme Future e First Conditional

---

# Le Forme del Futuro

## 1. **Will** (+ verbo base)

Decisioni **spontanee**, previsioni, offerte, promesse.

- I'll **help** you. → Ti aiuto. (offerta, deciso ora)
- She'll call you tomorrow. → Ti chiamerà domani. (previsione)
- He'll probably pass the exam. → Probabilmente passerà l'esame.

### Volontà / decisione al momento

| Affermazione | Negazione | Domanda |
|---|---|---|
| I**'ll** do it. | I **won't** do it. | **Will** you do it? |

> 💡 *Will* è uguale per tutte le persone: **I / He / She / We / They will**.

---

## 2. **Be going to** (+ verbo base)

Intenzioni, piani futuri, previsioni basate su **evidenza attuale**.

- I'm going to **learn** Rust. → Ho intenzione di imparare Rust.
- Look at those clouds — it's going to **rain**. → Guarda quelle nuvole, pioverà.

| Pronome | struttura |
|---|---|
| I am going to | I'm going to |
| He / She / It is going to | He's going to |
| We / They are going to | They're going to |

> ⚠️ *will* = **decisione presa adesso**, *be going to* = **piano già deciso prima**.
> - I**'ll** order the laptop now. (ora) vs I'm **going to order** a laptop. (già pensato)

---

## 3. **Present Continuous** — programmi e appuntamenti

- We're **meeting** the tutor on Monday. → (fissato in calendario)

---

## 4. **Present Simple** — orari e tabelle

- The train **leaves** at 9 a.m.
- The lecture **starts** at 10.
- Exams **begin** in June.

---

## Riepilogo

| Forma | Uso principale | Esempio |
|---|---|---|
| **will** | decisione spontanea, previsione | I'll send the email. |
| **be going to** | intenzione, previsione con evidenza | I'm going to refactor this. |
| **Present Continuous** | programma, appuntamento | I'm seeing the professor tomorrow. |
| **Present Simple** | orario fisso | The class starts at 9. |

# First Conditional — Realtà futura

> **If + Present Simple + will + verbo base**

## Struttura

> **If** clause (presente) + **main clause** (will)

> **If** we **fix** the bug, the app **will work**.

## Negazione

> If he **doesn't** come, we **will** start without him.
> Se non viene, inizieremo senza di lui.

## Domanda

> **Will** you help me **if** I **ask** you?
> What **will** happen **if** the server **crashes**?

---

## Regole fondamentali

> ⚠️ **Nella parte con _if_ si usa il PRESENTE, mai il futuro.**

| ❌ Errato | ✅ Corretto |
|---|---|
| If it **will rain**, we won't go. | If it **rains**, we won't go. |
| If you **will** help, thank you. | If you **help**, thank you. |
| If I **am** busy, I'll call you later. ✅ | (questo è corretto: presente) |

## Prima o seconda clausola?

| Posizione | Esempio |
|---|---|
| **if** in 2ª posizione (normale) | If it rains, **we won't go**. |
| **if** in 1ª posizione (inverso) | **If** it rains, we won't go. → We won't go **if** it rains. |

Nella forma inversa si mette una **virgola** dopo la clausola *if*.

---

## Espressioni equivalenti

| Espressione | Esempio |
|---|---|
| **if** | If you study, you'll pass. |
| **unless** | Unless you study, you won't pass. |
| **as long as** | As long as you study, you'll pass. |
| **provided (that)** | Provided you study, you'll pass. |
| **if not** | If you don't study, you won't pass. |

---

# First vs Second Conditional

| | First (realtà) | Second (irrealtà) |
|---|---|---|
| If | If + **present** | If + **past** |
| Main | **will** + base | **would** + base |
| Significato | probabile / reale | immaginario / improbabile |
| Esempio | If it rains, we **will** cancel. | If it rained, we **would** cancel. |
| Esempio | If I **were** richero → ❌ | If I **were** rich, I **would** buy a Mac. |

## Esercizi

| Frase | Risposta corretta |
|---|---|
| Look at the sky — it's ___ to rain. | **going** |
| I'll help you. → Negazione | **I won't help you.** |
| If you ___ the file, it will open. (close) | **close** |
| The train ___ at 7 tomorrow. (orario) | **leaves** |
| ___ you come to the lab? (offerta) | **Will** |
