# Aggettivi Comparativi e Superlativi

Servono a confrontare (comparativo) e a identificare il massimo (superlativo).

---

## Regole di formazione

### 1. Aggettivi CORTI (1 sillaba) → aggiungono -er / -est

| Positivo | Comparativo | Superlativo |
|---|---|---|
| fast | fast**er** | the fast**est** |
| cheap | cheap**er** | the cheap**est** |
| easy | eas**ier** | the eas**iest** |
| big | big**ger** | the big**gest** |
| happy | happ**ier** | the happ**iest** |

> ⚠️ **Attenzione alla grammatica:** raddoppiamo la consonante finale solo se l'ultima sillaba è **accentata** (big → bigger ✅, ma happy → happier ❌ perché la sillaba finale non è accentata).

### 2. Aggettivi LUNGHI (2+ sillabe) → more / the most

| Positivo | Comparativo | Superlativo |
|---|---|---|
| expensive | **more** expensive | the **most** expensive |
| difficult | **more** difficult | the **most** difficult |
| modern | **more** modern | the **most** modern |
| reliable | **more** reliable | the **most** reliable |

### 3. Irregolari (da imparare a memoria)

| Positivo | Comparativo | Superlativo |
|---|---|---|
| good | **better** | the **best** |
| bad | **worse** | the **worst** |
| far | **further/farther** | the **furthest/farthest** |
| little | **less** | the **least** |
| many / much | **more** | the **most** |

> ❌ *more better* → ✅ **better** (mai usare *more* con un comparativo già formato).

---

## Uso del comparativo

| Funzione | Struttura | Esempio |
|---|---|---|
| Superiore a | **more + agg + than** | This laptop is **faster than** that one. |
| Inferiore a | **less + agg + than** | This one is **less reliable than** mine. |
| Uguale | **as + agg + as** | He is **as experienced as** she is. |
| Diverso | **not as + agg + as** | It isn't **as fast as** the new model. |

> 💡 Nel **negativo** si usa quasi sempre **as ... as**, non *than*.

---

## Uso del superlativo

**the + agg -est / the most + agg**

- This is **the fastest** processor on the market.
- She is **the most competent** developer in the team.
- It was **the cheapest** laptop I could find.

---

## Parole utili con il comparativo

| Parola | Significato | Esempio |
|---|---|---|
| **much / far / a lot** | (solo comparativo) | This one is **much** faster. |
| **slightly / a bit / a little** | un po' | It's **a bit** slower. |
| **even** | persino | It's **even** more secure. |

---

## Esercizi

| Frase | Risposta corretta |
|---|---|
| My PC is ___ than yours. | **faster** |
| This is the ___ software in the office. | **most modern** |
| She is ___ than me. (good) | **better** |
| The results were ___ than expected. (bad) | **worse** |
| He isn't ___ as his colleague. (experienced) | **as experienced** |
