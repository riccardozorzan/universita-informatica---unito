# Articoli

## *A / An* — indeterminativi

Si usano per la **prima menzione**, per qualcosa di **nuovo o generico**.

| Articolo | Si usa davanti a | Esempio |
|---|---|---|
| **a** | consonante | a **computer**, a **university** |
| **an** | vocale | an **email**, an **hour**, an **ID** |

### La regola della vocale

Si decide in base al **suono**, non alla lettera:

| Parola | Suono | Articolo |
|---|---|---|
| hour | *au* (suono di vocale) | **an hour** |
| university | *iu* (suono di consonante) | **a university** |
| one | *w* (suono di consonante) | **a one** |
| European | *iu* | **a European** language |

> ⚠️ Le abbreviazioni si leggono **lettera per lettera**: **an** SQL file (*en-siu-cu-el*), **a** URL (*you-ar-el*), **an** HTML page.

---

## *The* — determinativo

Indica una cosa **specifica**, **già nota** o **unica**.

### Quando si usa

1. **Seconda menzione** → *I saw a dog. **The** dog was black.*
2. **Con superlativi** → *the fastest*
3. **Con *this / that / these / those*** → *this code, **the** problem*
4. **Unico nel genere** → *the sun, the internet, the manager*
5. **Strutture geografiche** → *the North, the Alps, **the** University of Turin*
6. **Nomi di fiumi, mari, catene** → *the Nile, the Mediterranean, the Alps*
7. **Patronimici** → *the Smiths*
8. **Strumenti musicali** → *play **the** piano*
9. **Arithmetic** → *the internet, the web*

### Quando **non** si usa

- Nomi **generici**: *~~The computers are expensive.* (sarebbe "quei computer specifici")
- Nomi **plurali generici**: *Students need laptops.*
- Nomi **astratti** in senso generale: *Life is short. **Honesty** matters. **Music** relaxes me.*
- Nomi di ** pasti, giochi, sport** senza *the*: *breakfast, lunch, dinner, chess, football*
- **Continenti, paesi, città**: *Italy, Europe, Turin*

---

## Zero article (nessun articolo)

Si usa per:

- **plurali generici**: *I like **computers**.*
- **nomi astratti generici**: *I like **music**.*
- **paesi e città**: *She lives in **Turin**. He studies in **Italy**.*
- **istituzioni generiche**: *He goes to **university**.* (= studiare all'università)
- **lingue**: *She speaks **English** and **French**.*
- **sport, pasti, giochi**: *They play **football**. I have **lunch** at one.*
- **malattie**: *He has **flu**. They have **cancer**.*

---

## Zero article vs *The* — la tabella chiave

| | Con zero | Con **the** |
|---|---|---|
| Musica | I like **music**. (generale) | **The music** is loud tonight. (specifica) |
| Internet | **Computers** are fast. | **The** internet is down. |
| Università | He is at **university**. | **The** University of Turin is big. |
| Computer | **Computers** are fast. | **The** computers in this lab are new. |

---

## Uso esteso di *the*

### Ordinali
> the first, the second, the third, the last

### Nomi propri di persone
> **Mario** Bianchi → ❌ *the Mario Bianchi* (già noto)

Ma con i titoli:
> **the** president Rossi, **the** professor Bianchi

---

## Possessivi con *the* vs senza

| | |
|---|---|
| ❌ | ~~**the** my laptop~~ |
| ❌ | ~~**the** his book~~ |
| ✅ | **my** laptop |
| ✅ | **his** book |

> Gli aggettivi possessivi **non** ammettono mai l'articolo *the*.

# Riepilogo completo dei pronomi

| Tipo | Esempi |
|---|---|
| **Soggetto** | I, you, he, she, it, we, they |
| **Oggetto** | me, you, him, her, it, us, them |
| **Possessivo (agg.)** | my, your, his, her, its, our, their |
| **Possessivo (pron.)** | mine, yours, his, hers, its, ours, theirs |
| **Riflessivo** | myself, yourself, himself, herself, itself, ourselves, yourselves, themselves |
| **Dimostrativi** | this, that, these, those |
| **Relativi** | who, whom, whose, which, that, where, what |
| **Indefiniti** | some, any, no, every, each, both, either, neither, one, many, much, few, little |
| **Interrogativi** | who, what, which, whose, where, when, why, how |

# Pronomi indefiniti — le coppie difficili

| Coppia | Con quantità | Senza quantità |
|---|---|---|
| **some** | some books | some students |
| **any** | any books | any students |
| **no** | no books | no students |
| **many** | many books | — |
| **much** | — | much time |
| **few** | a few books | — |
| **little** | — | a little time |

### *Some* / *any*

| | Affermativo | Negativo | Domanda |
|---|---|---|---|
| **some** | I have **some**. | (raro) | (raro) |
| **any** | (raro) | I don't have **any**. | Do you have **any**? |

### *A few* vs *A little*

| | Positivo | Negativo |
|---|---|---|
| **a few + plurale** | a few **errors** (alcuni) | **few** errors (pochi) |
| **a little + singolare/uncountable** | a little **time** (un po') | **little** time (poco) |

### *Both / either / neither*

| Pronome | Significato |
|---|---|
| **both** | entrambi (*of* per due persone) |
| **either** | uno qualsiasi dei due |
| **neither** | nessuno dei due |

# Esercizi

| Frase | Risposta corretta |
|---|---|
| She is ___ engineer. | **an** |
| I waited for ___ hour. | **an** |
| ___ internet is useful. | **The** |
| He studies ___ University of Turin. | **at the** |
| I love ___ music. | ~~the~~ (**no article**) |
| ___ computers are expensive. | ~~The~~ (**zero**) |
| I have ___ questions. (negazione) | **no** / **any** |
| This is ___ laptop (mine). | **my** |
